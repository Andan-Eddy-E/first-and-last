The law is on your side more often than you think

14 daysThe deadline for most NY landlords to return a deposit with an itemized statement. Miss it and they forfeit the right to keep any of it. GOL § 7-108
$50 or 5%The most a landlord can charge as a late fee, whichever is less, and only after rent is five days late. RPL § 238-a

How it works

  1. You type or paste. Both tools run entirely in your browser. Your lease, your landlord's name, and your address are never uploaded, stored, or seen by anyone.
  2. The rules do the reading. Each check is tied to a specific New York statute or city rule, and every result links to the source so you can read it yourself.
  3. You leave with something usable. A letter ready to send, a list of clauses to raise before signing, and clear next steps.

What this is and is not. First & Last provides legal information and self-help document tools. It is not a law firm, does not give legal advice, and using it does not create an attorney-client relationship. Rules change and every situation has details a tool cannot see. For advice about your situation, talk to a lawyer or a tenant organization such as Met Council on Housing or call 311 and ask for the Tenant Helpline.

First & Last is an independent project built in New York City. Legal information only, not legal advice. Covers New York State law and New York City rules as of September 2026.
