What a lease will not tell you

  1. Rent increases need advance notice. For an increase of 5% or more, or a non-renewal, the landlord owes you 30, 60, or 90 days of written notice depending on how long you have lived there. RPL § 226-c
  2. The landlord's broker cannot bill you. Since June 11, 2025, the FARE Act bars brokers who represent the landlord from charging the tenant, and every fee you owe must be disclosed in writing before signing. NYC DCWP
  3. Unlawful clauses do not void the whole lease. A clause the law will not enforce is usually just ignored by a court. Flagging it before signing is still worth doing, because it tells you how carefully the landlord follows the rules.
